## Frequently asked questions

**How many invoices are free?**
The first 3 invoices are free, with PDF and sharing. Pro unlocks unlimited invoices, recurring invoices, several businesses, all 8 templates, your logo on documents and reports.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open Settings in the App and tap *Restore Purchases*. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Invoice Lab → Cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**My invoices don't appear on my other device.**
Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account and iCloud is on for Invoice Lab (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device and in your private iCloud. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
