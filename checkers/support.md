## Frequently asked questions

**Which rules does the App use?**
Russian draughts on an 8×8 board: capturing is mandatory, pieces capture backwards, kings move any distance. Chess follows the standard rules, including castling, en passant and promotion. The full rules are in the App under Rules.

**How do I play with a friend?**
On one phone, choose *Two players* and pass the phone after each move. On two phones nearby, choose *Nearby*: one player taps *Create game*, the other taps *Find game*. Both phones need Wi-Fi or Bluetooth turned on; no internet is needed.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open Settings in the App and tap *Restore purchases*. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Checkers & Chess → Cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the current period. A lifetime purchase never renews.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
