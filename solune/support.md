## Frequently asked questions

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open Settings in the App and tap *Restore purchases*. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Solune → Cancel. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the current period.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
